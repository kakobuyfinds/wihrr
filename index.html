The quick answer

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. Useful fields include category, seller, listed price, measurements, available variants, QC references, and the date each detail was checked. Treat the list as a starting point for research: the original product listing remains the place to verify the item’s current details.

03 / The comparison desk

Keep the useful details.

For a Kakobuy product list, consistent fields make similar items easier to compare. Mark unverified information clearly and revisit it before ordering.

Suggested fields for your product research spreadsheet
Field What to record Why it helps
Original source Seller URL, item identifier, shop name, and check date. Distinguishes similar listings and makes later verification easier.
Selected variant Color, size, stated material, and any selectable version. Reduces confusion when photos or prices differ between variants.
Fit measurements Garment measurements or the relevant shoe size chart and units. Supports comparison with something that already fits you.
Price context Listed amount, currency, quantity, and which charges are excluded. Keeps a product price separate from an estimated delivered cost.
Photo evidence Listing images, available QC photos, visible defects, and photo date. Documents what you can inspect and what remains unknown.
Order conditions Current availability, accepted links, return terms, and restrictions. Identifies conditions that may affect whether you can complete the order.

05 / Read the photos carefully

A practical Kakobuy QC checklist

  • Confirm the photographed item matches the selected color and variant.
  • Inspect visible stitching, seams, shape, closures, and surface condition.
  • Check labels and hardware against the listing’s actual description.
  • Request measurements when fit cannot be judged from ordinary photos.
  • Consider lighting differences when comparing colors across photos.
  • Record issues before the relevant return or exchange deadline.

QC photos can reveal visible issues. They do not establish authenticity, long-term durability, or guaranteed fit.

06 / Brand-aware research

Know what you’re
actually comparing.

Researching Nike sneakers or Louis Vuitton (LV), Gucci, and Hermès fashion? Keep brand names separate from evidence. A familiar name in a title or image does not establish that an item is authentic or officially supplied.

Nike

Sneakers & sportswear

Check the stated model, colorway, size chart, and material details. Verify any authenticity claim through reliable purchase documentation.

Louis Vuitton / LV

Clothing & accessories

Compare dimensions, construction, material descriptions, and seller disclosures. Do not infer provenance from a monogram or product photograph.

Gucci

Apparel & headwear

Check garment measurements, hat sizing, composition, and care information. Review the exact listing rather than a similar-looking reference image.

Hermès

Jewelry & finishing pieces

Record dimensions, stated metal or material, closure details, and condition. Seek evidence for material, provenance, and authenticity claims.

08 / Straight answers

Before you
make the call.

Clear answers to common questions about product lists, measurements, QC photos, and shopping costs.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product research list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. Useful fields include category, seller, listed price, measurements, available variants, QC references, and the date each detail was checked.

Does a spreadsheet verify authenticity or seller reliability?

No. Inclusion in a spreadsheet does not verify authenticity, seller reliability, availability, or product quality. Check the original listing, seller information, return terms, and evidence for any authenticity claim.

How should I compare clothing and sneaker sizes?

Use the measurements on the specific listing rather than relying only on a size label. Compare clothing measurements with a garment you already own. For sneakers, compare foot length with the seller’s size chart and check whether measurements describe the foot, insole, or outsole.

What should I check in QC photos?

Review the item, selected variant, visible condition, stitching, shape, color, labels, and any requested measurements. QC photos can help identify visible issues, but they do not prove authenticity, long-term durability, or a perfect fit.

Is the listed product price the total delivered cost?

Usually not. Depending on the order, additional costs may include domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, currency conversion, and applicable taxes or duties. Confirm the current charges and shipping restrictions before paying.

Can every CAFANS listing be purchased through Kakobuy?

Do not assume universal compatibility. Check whether the original seller link is accepted and whether the product, destination, and shipping method are currently supported before placing an order.
